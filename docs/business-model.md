# Business Model

ISCO-08 1114: Senior Officials of Special-interest Organizations.

A senior official at a special-interest organization (trade union, industry association, advocacy group, professional society) operates an administrative office that handles member correspondence, meeting coordination, position-paper drafting, and conflict escalation.

This actor supports the administrative back-office workflow:

- **Member engagement**: Draft replies to member inquiries and correspondence
- **Meeting coordination**: Schedule member meetings and engagement activities
- **Position development**: Draft position papers for the official's review and decision
- **Conflict escalation**: Surface member disputes and concerns for official attention

The actor explicitly does NOT make binding organizational decisions, discipline members, or negotiate on the organization's behalf. All proposals are for the official's review and decision only.

See `operator-guide.md` for concrete usage patterns.
